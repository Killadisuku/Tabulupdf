Scanned PDF to Excel with AI Reading | TabulaPDF

Read a scanned PDF or photo of a table into Excel. AI reading is optional and sends that page to be processed. Digital PDFs stay in the browser.

https://tabulapdf.vercel.app/scanned-pdf-to-excel

Standard conversion never sees words that are not actually in the file. Scans, phone photos, and some “print to PDF” images need optical reading. When you start that mode, the page image goes to TabulaPDF’s extraction API and then to a vision model. It is not a local-only step.

The result comes back as rows and columns you can still edit. Skewed photos and faint type are less reliable than a flat scan. Check totals against the original before you trust the sheet.

If you can highlight the text with your mouse, skip this tool and use PDF to Excel. That path stays in the browser.

  1. Test for real text. Try to select a word. If you cannot, the file is a scan.
  2. Turn on AI reading. Only if the document is allowed to leave your computer.
  3. Proof the grid. Compare amounts to the page, then download Excel or CSV.

Questions

Is AI reading private to my browser?

No. The page image is uploaded so a vision model can read it. Digital PDF conversion is the mode that stays on your device.

What should I not send?

Do not send documents your policy forbids you to share with a processor: some payroll files, medical records, or client data under a contract.

Why did a number change?

Vision models misread decimals and similar digits. Always check the sheet against the page before you book the figures.
