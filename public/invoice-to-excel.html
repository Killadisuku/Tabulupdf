Questions

Does it read quantity and unit price?

Yes when those are real columns in the PDF. If the vendor printed them as one wrapped line, split the column before export.

Can I process more than one invoice?

Yes. Convert each PDF, then stack the sheets. There is no mailbox connection and no automatic vendor portal.

Is there a fee per invoice?

No. The converter is free and does not require an account.
