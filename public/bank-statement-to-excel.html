Questions

Are statement PDFs uploaded?

A normal digital statement is parsed in your browser. Do not turn on AI OCR unless you are willing to send that page to the extraction service.

Can I get separate debit and credit columns?

Often yes, when the PDF drew them as separate columns. If the bank printed one amount column with a sign, split or map it in the grid before export.

Will this file import to QuickBooks or Xero?

You get a clean spreadsheet. Map the columns in your accounting app’s CSV or Excel import. TabulaPDF does not log in to those products.
