Questions

Can I split every page into its own file?

Extract the ranges you need. For a long file, pull the ranges that matter rather than exploding every page.

Does this remove the rest of the document from my computer?

No. You download a new file. Your original stays where it was.

Then how do I get Excel?

Open the new PDF in PDF to Excel. Smaller files produce a cleaner table.
