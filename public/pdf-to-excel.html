Questions

Will I get a real Excel file or a CSV renamed as Excel?

You get a .xlsx workbook. CSV and TSV are separate exports if you would rather load the table into a database or a script.

Can several pages become one worksheet?

Yes. After the pages are read, use consolidate so repeated headers are not pasted into the middle of the sheet.

Does this upload my PDF?

Standard text-layer conversion runs in the browser. AI OCR is off unless you turn it on, and only then is a page image sent to be read.
